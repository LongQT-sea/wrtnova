Tag the LAN VLAN?

    
Instead of acting as normal access ports (LAN VLAN untagged/PVID), all LAN ports become trunk-only ports. Devices connected to these ports must support VLANs, such as a managed switch or a Linux/macOS computer with VLANs configured on its Ethernet port.
